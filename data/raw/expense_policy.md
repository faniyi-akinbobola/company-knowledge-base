## Overview

### Purpose
This policy ensures employees are fairly reimbursed for approved business expenses while maintaining fiscal responsibility and compliance with tax regulations.

### Guiding Principles
- **Business Purpose**: All expenses must have legitimate business need
- **Reasonable**: Costs should be moderate and justified
- **Documented**: Receipts and explanations required
- **Timely**: Submit within 30 days of expense date
- **Compliant**: Follow IRS and company guidelines

### Reimbursement Methods
- **Direct Deposit**: Fastest (2-3 business days after approval)
- **Paper Check**: Available upon request (5-7 business days)
- **Corporate Card Payment**: Some expenses paid directly by company

## Submission Process

### Using Expensify

**Step 1: Create Expense Report**
1. Log in to expensify.apextech.com (SSO)
2. Click "New Report"
3. Name report descriptively: "Travel - Chicago Client Visit - Feb 2026"

**Step 2: Add Expenses**
1. Click "Add Expense"
2. Upload receipt (photo or PDF)
3. Fill in:
   - Date of expense
   - Merchant name
   - Amount
   - Category (Travel, Meals, Supplies, etc.)
   - Business purpose (be specific!)
   - Attendees (if meal/entertainment)
4. Repeat for all expenses

**Step 3: Submit for Approval**
1. Review all expenses for accuracy
2. Add any additional notes
3. Click "Submit Report"
4. Report routed to your manager for approval

**Step 4: Track Status**
- Email notifications for approvals/rejections
- View status in Expensify dashboard
- Reimbursement via direct deposit upon approval

### Receipt Requirements

**Always Required:**
- Itemized receipt (not just credit card slip)
- Must show:
  - Date of purchase
  - Merchant name
  - Items purchased (not just total)
  - Payment method

**Receipt Substitutes (if lost):**
- Credit card statement + written explanation
- Screenshot of online transaction
- Manager approval for missing receipts

**No Receipt Needed:**
- Expenses under $25 (but encouraged)
- Mileage reimbursement (log required instead)
- Per diem meals (if applicable)

### Business Purpose Documentation

**Examples of GOOD Business Purpose:**
- "Lunch with Acme Corp CTO to discuss Q2 contract renewal"
- "Uber to/from airport for Client Summit in Austin"
- "Dinner with recruiting candidate - Software Engineer role"

**Examples of POOR Business Purpose:**
- "Lunch" (with whom? why?)
- "Taxi" (from where to where? for what?)
- "Conference" (which one? what relevance?)

**Be specific!** Finance may reject vague explanations.

### Submission Deadline

**Expense reports must be submitted within:**
- **30 days** of expense date (required)
- **60 days** = late fee applies (10% reduction)
- **90 days** = not reimbursable (exceptions require CFO approval)

**Tips to Avoid Delays:**
- Submit weekly (don't wait for month-end)
- Take receipt photos immediately
- Set calendar reminder to submit expenses

## Travel Expenses

### Airfare

**Policy:**
- Book economy class (domestic and international)
- Use approved travel booking tool: Egencia (egencia.apextech.com)
- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible
- Direct flights preferred (layovers okay if >$200 cheaper)

**Premium Class:**
- Business/First class requires VP approval
- Exception: Flights over 6 hours (Manager approval)

**Unused Tickets:**
If trip cancels, attempt to:
1. Cancel for credit (use for future travel)
2. Change to different dates
3. Request refund if available

Do not let tickets expire unused.

**Seat Selection & Baggage:**
- Complimentary seat selection: Reimbursable
- Paid seat upgrades: Not reimbursable (except extra legroom on 6+ hour flights)
- Checked baggage (first bag): Reimbursable
- Extra bags: Reimbursable if business need (bringing booth materials, etc.)

### Ground Transportation

**Preferred Methods:**
- **Airport Shuttle**: Use when available
- **Rideshare (Uber/Lyft)**: Standard service only (not Uber Black, Lyft Lux)
- **Taxi**: Acceptable
- **Public Transit**: Encouraged (subway, bus, train)
- **Personal Vehicle**: Mileage reimbursement (see below)

**Rental Cars:**
- Pre-approval required unless visiting multiple client sites
- Economy or mid-size vehicle only
- Decline rental company insurance (covered by company policy)
- Refuel before returning (to avoid inflated charges)

**Personal Vehicle Mileage:**
- Reimbursement rate: **$0.67/mile** (IRS standard rate 2026)
- Track mileage: Starting location, destination, miles, purpose
- Use Expensify mobile app to track automatically

**Parking & Tolls:**
- Reimbursable when necessary
- Prefer public transit if reasonable
- Valet parking: Only if no self-park option

### Lodging

**Hotel Selection:**
- Moderate business hotels (Courtyard, Hampton Inn, Holiday Inn, etc.)
- Book through Egencia or company-preferred vendors
- Limit: **$250/night** (San Francisco/NYC), **$200/night** (other cities)
- Higher rates require manager approval with justification

**Extended Stays (7+ nights):**
- Consider Airbnb or corporate housing
- Can be more cost-effective
- Must provide receipt and address

**Hotel Incidentals:**
- Wi-Fi: Reimbursable (if not included)
- Parking: Reimbursable
- Gym, movies, mini-bar, spa: NOT reimbursable

### Travel Insurance

**Company Coverage:**
Most needs covered by company insurance. Employees typically DO NOT need to purchase:
- Flight insurance
- Rental car insurance
- Travel medical insurance (if traveling on business)

**Exceptions:**
Trip cancellation insurance may be approved for expensive international travel (discuss with manager).

## Meals & Entertainment

### Business Meals

**Daily Limits (per person):**
- **Breakfast**: $15
- **Lunch**: $25
- **Dinner**: $50

**Group Meals:**
Multiply limit by number of attendees.
- Dinner for 4 people: $50 × 4 = $200 limit

**Higher-Cost Locations:**
SF, NYC, London: 25% increase allowed

**Requirements:**
- Itemized receipt
- List all attendees (names + companies)
- Business purpose clearly stated
- Alcohol limit: 2 drinks per person

### Per Diem (Alternative to Receipts)

**For multi-day travel, you may choose:**
- **Option A**: Submit individual meal receipts (up to daily limits)
- **Option B**: Per diem (no receipts needed)

**Per Diem Rates (2026):**
- **Domestic**: $75/day
- **International**: Varies by country (check GSA rates)

**Per Diem Rules:**
- No receipts required
- No additional meal reimbursements allowed
- Covers all meals for the day
- Elect per diem before trip (can't switch mid-trip)

### Entertainment & Client Gifts

**Client Entertainment:**
- Reasonable meals, events, or activities with clients
- Must have clear business purpose
- Alcohol: 2 drinks per person limit
- Document: Attendees, company, discussion topics

**Client Gifts:**
- Limit: $50 per client per year
- Examples: Branded swag, gift baskets, thank-you gifts
- No cash or cash equivalents (gift cards >$25)
- Manager approval for gifts >$50

**Team Celebrations:**
Not reimbursable via expense reports. Use team budget (manager has access).

## Professional Development

### Annual Stipend

**Amount:** $1,500 per employee per year
- Refreshes January 1
- Does not roll over (use it or lose it)

**Eligible Expenses:**
- Professional certifications (AWS, PMP, CPA, etc.)
- Online courses (Udemy, Coursera, etc.)
- Conference registration + travel
- Professional books and publications
- Membership dues (professional associations)
- Coaching or mentorship programs

**Not Eligible:**
- Degree programs (see Education Assistance Program separately)
- Personal development unrelated to job (yoga, cooking classes)
- Team-building activities (covered by separate budget)

### Approval Process

**Expenses Under $500:**
- Manager approval only
- Submit before purchasing

**Expenses Over $500:**
- Manager + Director approval
- Submit at least 2 weeks in advance
- Include: Course description, business relevance, cost breakdown

### Reimbursement Process

1. Get approval first (before purchasing)
2. Complete course/conference
3. Submit expense report with receipts
4. Include completion certificate (if applicable)

**Commitment:**
If you leave ApexTech within 12 months of expensive training (>$1,000), may be asked to reimburse on pro-rated basis. See [Employee Handbook](employee_handbook.md).

## Approval Requirements

### Standard Approval (Manager Only)

**Most expenses require only manager approval:**
- Travel under $1,000 per trip
- Meals within policy limits
- Office supplies under $50
- Professional development under $500

**Manager approval criteria:**
- Business need
- Within policy limits
- Reasonable cost
- Properly documented

### Higher-Level Approvals

| Expense Type | Amount | Approval Needed |
|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| Conference attendance | > $1,000 | Manager + Director |
| International travel | Any | Manager + HR (tax/legal) |
| Client entertainment | > $500 | Manager + VP |
| Equipment purchase | > $500 | Manager + IT/Procurement |
| Professional development | > $500 | Manager + Director |
| Premium airfare | Any | VP or higher |

### Pre-Approval vs. Post-Approval

**Pre-Approval REQUIRED:**
- Conference registration
- International travel
- Expensive training/certifications
- Equipment purchases
- Anything over $500

**Post-Approval Okay:**
- Standard business meals
- Routine travel (domestic client visits)
- Office supplies
- Ground transportation

**When in Doubt:** Get pre-approval. Better to ask than face rejection later.

## Reimbursement Timeline

### Processing Time

**After Approval:**
- **Direct Deposit**: 2-3 business days
- **Paper Check**: 5-7 business days

**If Delayed:**
Finance processes reimbursements twice weekly (Tuesdays, Thursdays). If submitted late in week, may process following week.

### Tracking Your Reimbursement

**In Expensify:**
- View report status: Submitted → Approved → Processing → Reimbursed
- Email notifications at each stage

**If Payment Delayed:**
1. Check Expensify status (may be pending approval)
2. Verify direct deposit info is correct
3. Contact finance: expenses@apextech.solutions

### Tax Implications

**Most Reimbursements:** Not taxable (business expenses)

**Exceptions (may be taxable):**
- Personal use of company vehicle
- Non-business meal portions
- Entertainment without business purpose

Finance will notify if expense is taxable. Included in W-2 if applicable.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

### Personal Expenses

**NOT Reimbursable:**
- ❌ Personal meals not related to business
- ❌ Personal entertainment (movies, concerts, non-business events)
- ❌ Gym memberships (unless part of specific benefit)
- ❌ Commuting to/from regular office
- ❌ Personal grooming (haircuts, toiletries)
- ❌ Clothing (unless required uniform/protective gear)
- ❌ Traffic/parking violations, tickets, fines
- ❌ Personal phone calls, internet for personal use
- ❌ Pet care, childcare
- ❌ Spouses/partners on business trips (unless approved exception)

### Excessive or Luxury Expenses

**NOT Reimbursable:**
- ❌ First-class airfare (without approval)
- ❌ Luxury hotels beyond policy limits
- ❌ Excessive meals (>$50 dinner without clients)
- ❌ Upgrade fees (seats, rental cars) without justification
- ❌ Mini-bar, room service, in-room movies
- ❌ Expensive client gifts (>$50 without approval)

### Lost or Stolen Items

**Generally NOT Reimbursable:**
- Personal items lost during travel
- Company equipment lost due to negligence

**Insurance May Cover:**
Check with HR regarding company insurance for lost company equipment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I expense alcohol?**
A: Yes, for client meals (2 drinks per person limit). Not for personal meals.

**Q: What if my expense was denied?**
A: Review denial reason in Expensify. Discuss with manager. Provide additional documentation if needed.

**Q: What if I forgot to submit an expense within 30 days?**
A: Submit anyway with explanation. Late fees may apply (10% reduction after 60 days). Expenses >90 days old rarely approved.

**Q: Can I expense meals when traveling alone?**
A: Yes, within daily limits (Breakfast $15, Lunch $25, Dinner $50).

**Q: What if my hotel is more expensive than the limit?**
A: Get manager pre-approval, or choose different hotel. Exceptions made for sold-out events or limited availability.

**Q: Can I use my personal credit card for business expenses?**
A: Yes. You'll be reimbursed upon submission. Some expenses (flights, hotels for large events) may be paid via corporate card.

**Q: What if I lost a receipt?**
A: Use credit card statement + explanation. May require manager approval. Avoid when possible.

**Q: Can I expense team dinners?**
A: Not via expense reports. Managers have team budget for team events. Discuss with your manager.

**Q: What if I traveled with my spouse? Can I expense their meals/lodging?**
A: No. Separate receipts required if possible. Spouse attendance requires executive approval (rare exceptions for multi-day events).
