## Overview

### Policy Statement
ApexTech Solutions supports flexible and remote work arrangements that enable employees to balance productivity with personal well-being while maintaining collaboration and company culture.

### Remote Work Options

**1. Hybrid Schedule** (Most Common)
- Work remotely **up to 3 days per week**
- Minimum 2 days in office (typically Tues/Wed/Thurs for team collaboration)
- Manager approval required

**2. Fully Remote**
- 100% remote work
- Limited to specific roles and circumstances
- Director-level approval required
- May require quarterly office visits

**3. Occasional Remote**
- Ad-hoc remote days as needed
- Manager notification (not formal approval)
- Examples: waiting for repair person, minor illness, focused work day

### Benefits of Remote Work
- Reduced commute time and stress
- Improved work-life balance
- Flexibility for personal appointments
- Focused time for deep work
- Reduced overhead costs

### Maintaining Company Culture
While we support remote work, we also value in-person collaboration:
- Team bonding and relationship building
- Spontaneous creativity and innovation
- Mentoring and knowledge transfer
- Company events and all-hands meetings

## Eligibility & Approval

### Eligibility Requirements

**To qualify for regular remote work:**
- [ ] Completed 90-day onboarding period (new hires)
- [ ] Met or exceeded performance expectations
- [ ] Role can be performed effectively remotely
- [ ] Demonstrated self-management and accountability
- [ ] Has reliable home internet and workspace
- [ ] Manager approval

**Exceptions:**
New hires may request remote work during hiring process. Approval at discretion of hiring manager and director.

### Role Suitability

**Generally Suitable for Remote:**
- Engineering (software development, DevOps)
- Product Management
- Design (UX/UI)
- Data & Analytics
- Marketing (content, digital, ops)
- Customer Success (account management)
- Finance & Accounting

**Limited Remote Suitability:**
- Customer-facing roles requiring in-person meetings
- Positions requiring specialized on-site equipment
- Early-career roles benefiting from in-person mentorship
- Roles requiring physical security presence

**Evaluated Case-by-Case:**
- Sales (mix of remote and customer visits)
- HR (some functions require in-person)
- Executive leadership (depends on role)

### Approval Process

**Requesting Remote Work Arrangement:**
1. Discuss with your manager (preferred approach and schedule)
2. Complete Remote Work Agreement form (HR Portal)
3. Manager reviews and approves/denies
4. HR and IT notified (for equipment and support)
5. Agreement reviewed quarterly

**Manager Considerations:**
- Employee performance and track record
- Team coverage and collaboration needs
- Business requirements and client expectations
- Equity across team members

**Denials:**
If request is denied, manager must provide explanation and may suggest alternative arrangements or conditions for reconsideration.

## Remote Work Schedules

### Hybrid Schedule Examples

**Option A: Fixed Days**
- Remote: Monday, Friday
- Office: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Predictable schedule, easy for team planning

**Option B: Flexible Days**
- Office minimum: 2 days/week
- Choose days based on meetings and deadlines
- Requires coordination with manager and team

**Option C: Team-Based**
- Entire team designates "team days" for in-office collaboration
- Remote work on other days
- Promotes synchronous teamwork

**Recommended In-Office Days:**
- **Tuesdays & Wednesdays**: Core collaboration days
- Sprint planning, team meetings, brainstorming
- Higher likelihood of teammates being in office

### Fully Remote Expectations

**For 100% remote employees:**
- Attend quarterly in-person team meetings (travel covered)
- Join annual company events (kickoff, summer retreat)
- Be available during core hours (see below)
- Maintain same productivity and communication standards
- Travel to office as needed for special projects (advance notice)

### Changing Your Schedule

**To modify remote schedule:**
1. Notify manager at least 1 week in advance (for permanent changes)
2. Update calendar to reflect work location ("WFH" vs. "Office")
3. Inform team via Slack
4. Adjust recurring meeting locations if needed

**Temporary Changes:**
Same-day remote work requests:
- Notify manager via Slack before 9:00 AM
- Update calendar
- Suitable for: minor illness, home emergencies, focused work needs

## Core Hours & Availability

### Core Hours Requirement

**Regardless of work location, employees must be available during:**
- **10:00 AM - 3:00 PM** (in your local time zone)

**During Core Hours:**
- Respond to Slack messages within 30 minutes
- Answer calls from manager or teammates
- Attend scheduled meetings
- Be actively working (not running errands, etc.)

**Outside Core Hours:**
Flexible start/end times are okay with manager approval
- Early bird: 7:00 AM - 3:00 PM
- Night owl: 11:00 AM - 7:00 PM

### Time Zone Considerations

**If working from different time zone:**
- Notify manager and HR (for payroll/tax implications)
- Core hours still apply (your local time)
- May need to accommodate some meetings in company HQ time
- Maximum 3-hour difference without executive approval

**International time zones:**
Requires special approval (see International & Extended Travel section)

### Indicating Availability

**Slack Status:**
- Use status to show location: "🏠 Working from home" or "🏢 In office"
- Set status when away: "🍽️ Lunch" "🧘 Break" "📅 Meeting"
- Mark as "Do Not Disturb" during focus time

**Calendar:**
- Block working hours on calendar
- Mark "Out of Office" for PTO
- Include location in meeting invites when relevant

## Equipment & Technology

### Company-Provided Equipment

**All Remote Employees Receive:**
- Laptop (Mac or Windows, your choice)
- Monitor (27" or 32" for full-time remote)
- Keyboard and mouse
- Webcam (if not built-in to laptop)
- Headset with microphone

- Docking station
- Power adapters

**Requesting Additional Equipment:**
Submit via IT Portal: itservices.apextech.com
- Second monitor

- External hard drive

### Home Office Stipend

**Full-Time Remote Employees:**
$500 one-time stipend for home office setup

**Eligible Expenses:**
- Desk
- Office chair
- Lighting (desk lamp, ring light)
- Cable management
- Printer (if role requires)

**Not Eligible:**
- Decorations
- General furniture
- Electronics beyond provided equipment

**How to Use:**
1. Purchase items
2. Submit receipts via Expensify
3. Reimbursement within 15 days
4. Receipts required, must be itemized

### Internet Requirements

**Minimum Specifications:**
- **Download Speed**: 25 Mbps
- **Upload Speed**: 10 Mbps
- **Latency**: <50ms
- **Reliability**: Stable connection (no frequent dropouts)

**Testing Your Connection:**
- Use speedtest.net
- Test during working hours (not late night)
- If below minimum, contact IT for solutions

**Internet Reimbursement:**
- Full-time remote: $75/month internet stipend
- Hybrid remote: Not eligible (unless approved exception)
- Submit via expense report monthly

### VPN & Security

**Required:**
- Connect to VPN before accessing company systems
- Use company-issued devices only (no personal laptops)
- Enable FileVault/BitLocker encryption (pre-configured)
- Install security updates promptly

**See [Security Policy](security_policy.md) and [IT Support Guide](it_support_guide.md) for details.**

## Home Office Setup

### Workspace Requirements

**Must Have:**
- Dedicated workspace (not bed, couch)
- Proper desk and ergonomic chair
- Good lighting (natural + task lighting)
- Quiet environment for calls
- Stable internet connection
- Secured/locked when not in use (if handling confidential data)

**Nice to Have:**
- Separate room/office
- Standing desk option
- Plants and personalization
- Dual monitors

### Ergonomics

**To prevent injury and fatigue:**
- Monitor at eye level (use laptop stand)
- Elbows at 90° when typing
- Feet flat on floor
- Back supported by chair
- Take breaks every hour (stand, stretch, walk)

**Resources:**
- Ergonomic assessment guide: apextech.notion.so/ergonomics
- Request assessment from HR if experiencing discomfort

### Professional Background for Video Calls

**Recommended:**
- Clean, uncluttered background
- Neutral colors
- Good lighting on your face (not backlit window)
- Virtual backgrounds acceptable (but natural preferred)

**Avoid:**
- Messy or distracting backgrounds
- Inappropriate items in view
- Overly personal content (keep it professional)

## Communication & Collaboration

### Over-Communicate When Remote

**Best Practices:**
- Start day with Slack check-in to team
- Update status when away from desk
- Document decisions in writing (Notion, Jira comments)
- Use video for meetings (not just audio)
- Proactively share progress on projects

### Meeting Etiquette for Remote Workers

**Video Meetings:**
- Camera ON for team meetings (builds connection)
- Camera optional for large all-hands or webinars
- Mute when not speaking
- Use virtual backgrounds if needed
- Professional appearance (dress as you would for office)

**Audio Quality:**
- Use headset (not laptop microphone)
- Find quiet space
- Mute background noise (pets, kids, construction)

**Being Present:**
- Close other tabs/apps to minimize distractions
- Actively participate (don't just listen passively)
- Use chat for questions/comments

### Collaboration Tools

**Primary Communication:**
- **Slack**: Quick messages, team channels, status updates
- **Zoom**: Video meetings, screen sharing, collaboration
- **Google Meet**: Alternative for certain meetings
- **Email**: Formal communication, external parties

**Project Management:**
- **Jira**: Tasks, sprints, bugs
- **Notion**: Documentation, wikis, project pages
- **Google Drive**: File storage and sharing

**See [Company Wiki](company_wiki.md) for detailed tool usage guidelines.**

### Staying Connected to Team

**Strategies:**
- Schedule virtual coffee chats
- Join #random and team social Slack channels
- Participate in virtual team events
- Come to office on designated "team days"
- Attend company events (all-hands, offsites, parties)

**Loneliness/Isolation:**
If feeling disconnected, talk to your manager or reach out to People Ops. We can help:
- Connect you with Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)
- Arrange co-working opportunities
- Adjust remote schedule to increase office time

## Performance Expectations

### Same Standards, Different Location

**Remote work is a privilege, not a right.** Performance expectations remain the same whether in office or remote:
- Meet deadlines and deliverables
- Attend meetings and be responsive
- Collaborate effectively with team
- Produce high-quality work
- Demonstrate ownership and accountability

### Measuring Success

**Managers evaluate based on:**
- **Output**: Work completed, goals achieved
- **Quality**: Standards met, feedback from stakeholders
- **Communication**: Responsiveness, clarity, proactiveness
- **Collaboration**: Team support, knowledge sharing
- **Professionalism**: Meeting etiquette, availability during core hours

**Not based on:**
- Hours logged (we're results-oriented, not time-tracking)
- Location (office vs. remote doesn't matter if output is strong)

### Performance Issues

**If performance declines while remote:**
- Manager will address in 1:1
- May require return to office (temporarily or permanently)
- Performance improvement plan if needed
- Remote work is revocable if expectations not met

**Common Remote Work Challenges:**
- Distractions at home → Dedicated workspace, "do not disturb" boundaries
- Isolation → Proactive communication, virtual coffee chats
- Overworking → Set boundaries, log off at end of day
- Communication gaps → Over-communicate, document decisions

## International & Extended Travel

### "Workcations" (Working While Traveling)

**Short-Term (< 2 weeks):**
- Manager approval required
- Must maintain core hours availability
- Reliable internet required
- Consider time zone differences
- VPN required for company system access

**Example:**
Visiting family out of state for 1 week, working remotely during weekdays.

**How to Request:**
1. Discuss with manager (at least 2 weeks notice)
2. Submit PTO for any non-working days
3. Confirm internet access and workspace at destination
4. Update Slack status and calendar with location

### Extended Domestic Travel (2+ weeks)

**Requirements:**
- HR approval (potential tax implications)
- Confirm legal and tax compliance for that state
- Ensure no impact to work quality or availability

**How to Request:**
1. Submit request via HR Portal (4+ weeks notice)
2. Provide: Dates, location, reason
3. HR reviews tax/legal implications
4. Approval or denial with explanation

### International Travel

**Working from Another Country:**
- **Complex tax and legal implications**
- Requires executive approval
- Advance notice: 8+ weeks
- Generally limited to 2-4 weeks per year

**Process:**
1. Submit request to HR and Legal
2. Provide: Country, duration, business justification
3. Legal review (visa, tax, compliance)
4. Approval contingent on legal clearance
5. May require additional insurance

**Tax Implications:**
Working internationally can create tax obligations for both employee and company. Carefully review before requesting.

**Exceptions:**
Business travel to meet customers or attend conferences does not require this process.

## Security & Data Protection

### Remote Work Security Requirements

**Must Do:**
- Use VPN when accessing company systems
- Lock screen when away from computer (even at home)
- Use company devices only (no personal laptops)
- Secure home Wi-Fi (strong password, WPA2/WPA3)
- Shred confidential documents (or secure locked filing)
- Don't work from public spaces on confidential data

**Must Not Do:**
- ❌ Share workspace with non-employees during sensitive work
- ❌ Allow family/friends to use company devices
- ❌ Leave company devices in car or unsecured
- ❌ Discuss confidential information where others can overhear
- ❌ Use public Wi-Fi without VPN

**See [Security Policy](security_policy.md) for complete guidelines.**

### Home Network Security

**Securing Your Wi-Fi:**
1. Change default router password
2. Use WPA3 or WPA2 encryption (not WEP)
3. Don't share Wi-Fi password widely
4. Disable WPS (Wi-Fi Protected Setup)
5. Keep router firmware updated

**If family members share your network:**
- Keep company devices separate (don't let others use them)
- Use VPN for added security
- Consider separate guest network for visitors

### Confidential Meetings at Home

**When handling sensitive information:**
- Close doors/windows if neighbors nearby
- Use headphones for confidential calls
- Mute yourself when not speaking (background noise)
- Ensure others in home know not to interrupt

**Video Call Background:**
No sensitive documents or whiteboards visible in background.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I work remotely full-time?**
A: Depends on your role. Some roles support 100% remote, others require hybrid. Discuss with your manager.

**Q: Do I need to use vacation days for a "workcation"?**
A: No, as long as you're working full-time and available during core hours. PTO only needed for non-working days.

**Q: What if my internet goes down while working remotely?**
A: Notify manager immediately. Options: work from office, co-working space, or use PTO if unable to work.

**Q: Can I work from coffee shops?**
A: For non-confidential work, yes (with VPN). For sensitive data or calls, use private space.

**Q: Who pays for my home internet?**
A: Full-time remote employees receive $75/month stipend. Hybrid employees generally do not (unless special approval).

**Q: What if I move to a different state?**
A: Notify HR immediately. May have tax/legal implications. Approval required before relocating.

**Q: Can I work remotely while on vacation?**
A: We discourage mixing work and vacation. Use PTO to fully unplug. If you must work, request "workcation" approval.

**Q: How do I access a printer when remote?**
A: Print and scan via office printer when in office. For home printing, request printer via IT Portal (role-dependent).

**Q: What if I'm more productive at home and want to work remotely every day?**
A: Discuss with manager. We balance individual productivity with team collaboration needs. May be possible depending on role.

**Q: Can my manager require me to come to office even on my remote days?**
A: Yes, for important meetings, team events, or business needs. Reasonable notice should be provided.
