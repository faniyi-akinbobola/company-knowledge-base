## Welcome to ApexTech Solutions

**Congratulations on joining the ApexTech team!**

We're excited to have you join us on our mission to empower businesses through cloud technology and AI innovation. This guide will help you navigate your first weeks and set you up for success.

### What to Expect
Your onboarding experience includes:
- **Structured First Week**: Guided orientation covering tools, people, and processes
- **90-Day Ramp Plan**: Clear milestones with your manager
- **Buddy Program**: Assigned peer for day-to-day questions
- **Continuous Support**: Regular check-ins with HR and your manager

### Your Onboarding Team
You'll be supported by:
- **Your Manager**: Primary guide for role-specific onboarding
- **Onboarding Buddy**: Peer mentor for culture and practical questions
- **People Ops Specialist**: Administrative support and benefits enrollment
- **IT Support**: Technical setup and access

## First 30-60-90 Days

Your manager will create a detailed ramp plan, but here's what to expect:

### First 30 Days: Learn
**Focus:** Absorb information, build relationships, understand context

**Goals:**
- Complete all mandatory training
- Understand team structure and how your role fits
- Learn core tools and systems
- Build foundational knowledge of products/customers
- Identify quick wins and low-hanging fruit

**Typical Activities:**
- Shadowing team members
- Reading documentation
- Attending meetings as an observer
- Asking lots of questions
- Small, guided contributions

**Checkpoint Meeting (Day 30):**
- Manager reviews progress against onboarding plan
- Discuss what's clicking vs. what needs more support
- Adjust ramp plan if needed
- Set goals for next 30 days

### Days 31-60: Contribute
**Focus:** Apply knowledge, take on real work, build credibility

**Goals:**
- Own small projects or features independently
- Contribute meaningfully to team meetings
- Develop deeper expertise in your area
- Build relationships across departments
- Demonstrate alignment with company values

**Typical Activities:**
- Leading smaller initiatives
- Presenting work to team
- Collaborating on cross-functional projects
- Attending customer meetings or user research
- Proposing process improvements

**Checkpoint Meeting (Day 60):**
- Assess readiness for full role responsibilities
- Gather feedback from teammates and stakeholders
- Discuss career growth interests
- Set quarterly goals (OKRs)

### Days 61-90: Own & Influence
**Focus:** Full autonomy, strategic thinking, team impact

**Goals:**
- Operate independently with minimal oversight
- Drive projects from ideation to completion
- Mentor newer team members
- Contribute to team strategy and planning
- Identify opportunities for innovation

**Typical Activities:**
- Leading significant projects
- Participating in sprint/roadmap planning
- Sharing knowledge through documentation or training
- Representing team in cross-functional initiatives
- Proposing new ideas and improvements

**90-Day Review:**
- Formal performance discussion with manager
- 360° feedback from teammates
- Assessment against role expectations
- Compensation review (if applicable)
- Career development planning for next 6-12 months

### Success Metrics
You're successfully onboarded when you can:
- Work independently without constant guidance
- Know who to ask when you need help
- Contribute meaningfully to team goals
- Understand how your work impacts customers
- Navigate company tools and processes confidently
- Embody ApexTech values in your daily work

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When will I get paid?**
A: ApexTech pays biweekly on Fridays. Your first paycheck will be prorated based on your start date. Direct deposit takes 1 pay cycle to activate; your first check may be paper.

**Q: How do I request time off?**
A: Use BambooHR to submit PTO requests. Notify your manager as well. See [HR Policies](hr_policies.md) for details.

**Q: Can I start working remotely right away?**
A: We recommend your first 2 weeks in-office to meet people and onboard effectively. After that, follow the [Remote Work Policy](remote_work_policy.md) (typically up to 3 days/week remote).

**Q: What if I don't understand something?**
A: Ask! No question is too basic. Use your buddy, manager, #ask-anything channel, or onboarding office hours.

**Q: When can I access 401k and benefits?**
A: 401k is available immediately; you can enroll on Day 1. Health insurance starts the 1st of the month following your start date (or immediately if you start on the 1st).

**Q: How do I expense things?**
A: Use Expensify for all business expenses. Your manager must pre-approve expenses over $100. See [Expense Policy](expense_policy.md).

**Q: What's the deal with equipment?**
A: You'll get a laptop on Day 1. Additional equipment (monitors, keyboard, etc.) can be requested via IT portal. Home office setup allowance is $500 for remote employees.

**Q: Can I work from a different city/country temporarily?**
A: Short-term (< 2 weeks): usually yes with manager approval. Long-term: requires HR approval due to tax implications. See [Remote Work Policy](remote_work_policy.md).
