## Mandatory Training

All employees must complete these courses within **30 days of hire**.

### 1. Cybersecurity Awareness Training
**Duration:** ~2 hours  
**Platform:** KnowBe4 (link sent via email)

**Topics Covered:**
- Password security and multi-factor authentication
- Identifying phishing and social engineering attacks
- Data protection and classification
- Safe internet browsing and email practices
- Reporting security incidents
- Mobile device security
- Physical security best practices

**Completion:**
- Interactive modules with quizzes
- Must score 80% or higher to pass
- Certificate issued upon completion
- **Annual refresh required**

**Monthly Phishing Simulations:**
After initial training, you'll receive simulated phishing emails monthly. This tests your awareness and helps identify areas for additional training.

**What happens if you click?**
- Immediate redirect to training video
- No penalties (it's a learning opportunity)
- Repeated failures may require additional 1:1 training

### 2. Workplace Conduct & Anti-Harassment Training
**Duration:** ~1 hour  
**Platform:** BambooHR

**Topics Covered:**
- Code of conduct review
- Defining harassment and discrimination
- Creating an inclusive workplace
- Recognizing and reporting inappropriate behavior
- Bystander intervention
- Company investigation process
- Non-retaliation policy

**Format:**
- Video scenarios with decision points
- Real-world case studies
- Interactive quizzes
- Certificate upon 100% completion

**Why This Matters:**
Creating a respectful, inclusive workplace is everyone's responsibility. This training ensures we all understand expectations and how to support each other.

**Annual Recertification:** Required every 12 months.

### 3. Data Privacy & GDPR Compliance Training
**Duration:** ~1 hour  
**Platform:** BambooHR

**Topics Covered:**
- What is personal data (PII)?
- GDPR requirements and implications
- CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- Data minimization principles
- Customer rights (access, deletion, portability)
- Handling data subject requests
- Privacy by design
- Cross-border data transfers
- Breach notification requirements

**Role-Specific Modules:**
- **Engineering**: Secure coding, data retention
- **Sales/CS**: Customer data handling, consent
- **Marketing**: Email privacy, opt-out requirements

**Certification:** Valid for 1 year, annual renewal required.

### 4. Company Orientation (New Hires Only)
**Duration:** 2 hours  
**Format:** Live session during first week

**Agenda:**
- Welcome and introductions
- Company history and mission
- Product overview and demos
- Organizational structure
- Core values in action
- Benefits overview
- Q&A with leadership

**Includes:**
- Virtual tour of offices (for remote employees)
- Meet the executive team
- Introduction to Employee Resource Groups
- Swag distribution

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if I don't complete mandatory training on time?**
A: You'll receive reminders. After 30 days overdue, HR may restrict access to systems and escalate to your manager. Complete ASAP to avoid impact on performance review.

**Q: Can I take courses outside of work hours?**
A: Yes, but not required. You can use work time for approved learning (discuss with manager). Balance learning with deliverables.

**Q: How do I request training not in the catalog?**
A: Submit request via learning portal with: Course name, provider, cost, business justification. Manager + L&D approve.

**Q: Does the $1,500 professional development budget roll over?**
A: No, it resets January 1 each year. Use it or lose it!

**Q: Can I get certified in something unrelated to my current role?**
A: Possibly, if there's a career path connection (e.g., engineer wants product management skills). Discuss with manager.

**Q: What if I fail a certification exam?**
A: Company pays for first attempt. Second attempt requires manager approval. Third attempt is self-funded.

**Q: Is training time counted as work time?**
A: Yes! Mandatory and approved training is work. Don't use PTO.

**Q: Can I attend conferences just to learn, or do I need to present/recruit?**
A: Both are valid. Learning is valuable. Speaking/recruiting earns extra perks, but not required.
