## Benefits Eligibility

### Full-Time Employees
**Eligible for all benefits** outlined in this document.
- Regular full-time status (40 hours/week)
- Benefits effective first of month following start date
- Example: Start date Feb 15 → Benefits active March 1

### Part-Time Employees
**Pro-rated benefits** based on hours worked:
- PTO: Pro-rated based on schedule
- Health insurance: Available if working 30+ hours/week
- 401(k): Eligible immediately
- Other benefits: Varies (discuss with HR)

### Contractors/Temporary Workers
**Not eligible** for company benefits.
- May purchase individual health insurance
- Not eligible for PTO, 401(k) employer match, etc.

### Dependents Eligible for Coverage
- Legal spouse or domestic partner
- Children up to age 26
- Disabled dependents (no age limit with proper documentation)

## Health Insurance

ApexTech offers comprehensive medical coverage through **Blue Cross Blue Shield**.

### Plan Options (2026)

#### **Option 1: PPO Plan (Preferred Provider Organization)**
**Best for:** Flexibility, frequent medical needs

**Premiums (monthly, employee portion):**
- Employee only: $120
- Employee + Spouse: $280
- Employee + Child(ren): $260
- Family: $380

**Coverage:**
- In-network deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family
- Out-of-network deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
- Co-insurance: 80/20 (plan pays 80%)
- Out-of-pocket max: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family
- Primary care visit: $25 copay
- Specialist visit: $50 copay
- Urgent care: $75 copay
- ER visit: $250 copay

**Advantages:**
- See any doctor (in or out of network)
- No referrals needed for specialists
- Broad provider network

#### **Option 2: HDHP (High Deductible Health Plan) with HSA**
**Best for:** Healthy individuals, want to save on premiums

**Premiums (monthly, employee portion):**
- Employee only: $60
- Employee + Spouse: $160
- Employee + Child(ren): $140
- Family: $220

**Coverage:**
- Deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
- Co-insurance: 80/20 after deductible
- Out-of-pocket max: $5,000 individual / $10,000 family
- Preventive care: $0 (covered 100%)
- All other services: Pay deductible first, then 80/20

**HSA (Health Savings Account):**
- Company contributes: $750/year (employee only) or $1,500/year (family)
- You can contribute additional (2026 limits: $4,150 individual / $8,300 family)
- Tax-free contributions, growth, and withdrawals (for medical expenses)
- Funds roll over year to year (you keep it even if you leave)
- Can invest HSA funds like a 401(k)

**Advantages:**
- Lower premiums
- Tax-advantaged HSA savings
- Good for healthy individuals

### What's Covered (Both Plans)

**Preventive Care (100% covered, no deductible):**
- Annual physical exams
- Immunizations
- Screenings (cancer, diabetes, cholesterol)
- Well-child visits
- Annual OB-GYN exams

**Medical Services:**
- Hospital stays
- Surgery and procedures
- Emergency care
- Lab work and X-rays
- Mental health services
- Prescription drugs (see below)

**Prescription Drug Coverage:**
- Generic: $10 copay
- Preferred brand: $30 copay
- Non-preferred brand: $60 copay
- Specialty drugs: 20% coinsurance (up to $200/month)
- Mail-order pharmacy: 90-day supply for 2× copay

### Finding Providers

**Provider Search:** bcbs.com/find-a-doctor
- Enter ZIP code
- Filter by "ApexTech Solutions" plan
- Check if in-network (lower costs)

**Telemedicine:**
- MDLIVE available 24/7
- Video/phone consultations
- $0 copay for PPO, $49 for HDHP (before deductible met)
- Prescriptions sent to your pharmacy

## Retirement Savings (401k)

### Plan Overview
ApexTech offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan through **Fidelity**.

**Eligibility:**
- Immediate (enroll on day 1)
- All employees (full-time and part-time)

### Company Match
**Generous employer match to help you save:**
- ApexTech matches **100% of first 6%** you contribute
- Example: You contribute 6% of salary → Company adds another 6%
- Vest immediately (it's your money from day 1)

**Example:**
- Salary: $100,000
- You contribute: 6% = $6,000
- Company match: 6% = $6,000
- **Total annual contribution: $12,000**

### Contribution Limits (2026)
- **Employee contribution limit**: $23,500/year
- **Age 50+ catch-up**: Additional $7,500
- **Total limit** (employee + employer): $70,000

### Investment Options
- **Target Date Funds**: Automatic rebalancing based on retirement year
- **Index Funds**: Low-cost broad market exposure (S&P 500, Total Market)
- **Bond Funds**: Lower risk, steady returns
- **International Funds**: Global diversification
- **Company Stock**: Option to invest in ApexTech (limit: 10% of portfolio)

**Free Financial Advisory:**
Fidelity provides free consultations with financial advisors (in-person or phone). Highly recommended!

### Roth 401(k) Option
**Traditional vs. Roth:**
- **Traditional**: Pre-tax contributions (reduces taxable income now), taxed in retirement
- **Roth**: After-tax contributions (no tax benefit now), tax-free withdrawals in retirement
- **Can split** between both

**Who should choose Roth?**
- Younger employees (expect higher tax bracket in retirement)
- Want tax-free growth and withdrawals

### Accessing Your 401(k)
- **Portal**: netbenefits.com
- **Mobile app**: Fidelity Mobile
- **Customer service**: 1-800-343-3548

**Loans & Withdrawals:**
- Can borrow up to 50% of balance (repay with interest)
- Hardship withdrawals available (medical, housing, education)
- Early withdrawal penalty: 10% + taxes (if under 59½)

## Life & Disability Insurance

### Basic Life Insurance (Company-Paid)
**Coverage:** 1× annual salary
- No cost to you
- Automatic enrollment
- Beneficiary designation required

**Example:**
- Salary: $80,000 → Coverage: $80,000 payout to beneficiary

### Supplemental Life Insurance (Employee-Paid, Optional)
**Additional coverage** available for purchase:
- Employee: Up to 5× salary (max $500,000)
- Spouse: Up to $250,000
- Child: $10,000 per child

**Rates:** Based on age, typically $0.20-$0.50 per $1,000 coverage

**When to Add:**
- Have dependents
- Mortgage or large debts
- Want to ensure family is financially secure

### Short-Term Disability (Company-Paid)
**Covers:** Injury or illness preventing you from working
- **Benefit:** 60% of salary
- **Waiting period:** 14 days
- **Duration:** Up to 90 days
- Examples: Surgery recovery, serious illness, pregnancy complications

### Long-Term Disability (Company-Paid)
**Covers:** Extended disability beyond 90 days
- **Benefit:** 60% of salary
- **Duration:** Until age 65 or recovery
- **Waiting period:** 90 days (after short-term ends)
- Examples: Chronic illness, severe injury

**Supplemental LTD (Employee-Paid):**
Option to increase benefit to 70% of salary.

## Enrollment & Changes

### New Hire Enrollment

**Timeline:**
- Benefits overview during onboarding (Week 1)
- Enrollment deadline: **30 days from start date**
- Benefits effective: 1st of month following start date

**How to Enroll:**
1. Visit benefits.apextech.com
2. Review plan options
3. Select coverage (health, dental, vision, life, disability)
4. Designate beneficiaries (life insurance, 401k)
5. Set up 401(k) contributions
6. Submit enrollment

**Need Help?**
- Benefits team available: benefits@apextech.solutions
- Office hours: Tuesdays/Thursdays 2-4 PM
- Virtual Q&A sessions for new hires (check calendar)

### Annual Open Enrollment

**When:** November 1-30 every year
**Effective:** January 1 (next year)

**During Open Enrollment:**
- Review and change benefit selections
- Add/remove dependents
- Adjust 401(k) contributions
- Enroll in FSA/HSA (if applicable)

**If You Don't Enroll:**
Current elections roll over (except FSA—must re-elect annually).

### Qualifying Life Events (QLE)

**You can change benefits mid-year if you experience:**
- Marriage or divorce
- Birth or adoption of child
- Death of spouse or dependent
- Loss of other coverage
- Change in employment status (full-time to part-time, etc.)

**Deadline:** **30 days** from event date

**How to Make Changes:**
1. Submit QLE form via benefits portal
2. Provide documentation (marriage certificate, birth certificate, etc.)
3. HR reviews and approves
4. Changes effective date of event (or first of month following)

### COBRA (Continuing Coverage)

**If you leave ApexTech:**
- Eligible for COBRA continuation (health, dental, vision)
- Duration: Up to 18 months
- Cost: Full premium (company no longer subsidizes) + 2% admin fee
- Enrollment deadline: 60 days from termination

**Alternatives to COBRA:**
- Healthcare.gov (ACA marketplace)
- Spouse's employer coverage
- Medicaid (if eligible)

COBRA is expensive—explore alternatives first.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When do my benefits start?**
A: First of the month following your start date (or immediately if you start on the 1st).

**Q: Can I waive health insurance?**
A: Yes, if you have other coverage (spouse's plan, parent's plan if under 26, etc.). You'll receive a small stipend (~$100/month) for waiving.

**Q: What if I miss the enrollment deadline?**
A: You'll have to wait until next open enrollment (or have a qualifying life event). Choose carefully!

**Q: How much should I contribute to 401(k)?**
A: At minimum, contribute 6% to get full company match (free money!). Ideally, aim for 10-15% total savings rate.

**Q: Can I change my 401(k) contribution anytime?**
A: Yes! Update via netbenefits.com anytime. Changes take effect next payroll cycle.

**Q: What happens to my benefits if I go on leave (parental, medical, etc.)?**
A: Benefits continue during paid leave. For unpaid leave, contact HR—you may need to pay your portion of premiums.

**Q: Are benefits available for part-time employees?**
A: Some benefits yes (401k, pro-rated PTO). Health insurance if working 30+ hours/week. Check with HR.

**Q: Can my domestic partner be covered?**
A: Yes, if you complete domestic partner affidavit and provide proof of partnership.

**Q: What if my spouse also works at ApexTech?**
A: You can both enroll in benefits separately, or one can cover both under their plan. Choose what's most cost-effective.
