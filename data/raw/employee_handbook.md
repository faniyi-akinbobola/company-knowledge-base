## Core Values

Our five core values guide decision-making at every level of the organization. These aren't just words on a wall—they're embedded in our hiring, performance reviews, and daily operations.

### 1. Innovation
**We embrace bold ideas and continuous improvement.**

**In Action:**
- Engineers get "Innovation Fridays" to work on passion projects
- Quarterly hackathons with prizes for best innovations
- Fail-forward mentality: We celebrate smart risks even when they don't pan out
- 10% of engineering time allocated to technical debt and experimentation

**Example:** Our AI autocomplete feature began as a hackathon project and now saves customers 15+ hours per week.

### 2. Integrity
**We do the right thing, even when it's hard.**

**In Action:**
- Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
- We admit mistakes publicly and fix them quickly
- Ethical AI principles guide product development
- Whistleblower protections for reporting misconduct

**Example:** When we discovered a pricing error that undercharged customers, we honored those rates rather than retroactively billing the difference.

### 3. Collaboration
**We win together and support each other's success.**

**In Action:**
- Cross-functional project teams (not siloed departments)
- Peer recognition program with monthly shoutouts
- Open Slack channels where anyone can ask questions
- Regular "lunch and learn" sessions across departments

**Example:** Our product team holds weekly office hours where engineers, designers, and support staff collaboratively solve customer problems.

### 4. Customer Focus
**Our customers' success is our success.**

**In Action:**
- Every employee spends one day per year in customer support
- NPS (Net Promoter Score) is a company-wide KPI
- Customer feedback directly influences product roadmaps
- 24/7 support with <15 minute response time SLA

**Example:** We delayed a major product launch by 3 weeks based on beta tester feedback that a feature needed improvement.

### 5. Continuous Learning
**We invest in growth—both personal and professional.**

**In Action:**
- $1,500 annual professional development stipend per employee
- Monthly "Knowledge Share" sessions where employees teach skills
- Support for conference attendance and speaking engagements
- Access to Udemy, Coursera, and O'Reilly Learning platforms
- Mentorship program pairing junior and senior employees

**Example:** Our CTO started as a junior developer and grew through our internal leadership development program.

## Performance Management

### Philosophy
We believe performance management should be continuous, transparent, and focused on growth—not just evaluation. Our system balances individual goals with team objectives.

### Performance Review Cycle

**Bi-Annual Formal Reviews**
- **Mid-Year Check-In**: June (lightweight, developmental focus)
- **Annual Review**: December (comprehensive, influences compensation)

**Continuous Feedback**
- Monthly 1:1s with your manager
- Quarterly goal check-ins
- Peer feedback anytime via Lattice platform
- Real-time recognition through Bonusly

### Review Components

**1. Goal Achievement (40%)**
Evaluation against quarterly OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)
- Set collaboratively with manager each quarter
- Should include both business outcomes and personal development goals
- Graded on a 5-point scale (Exceeds, Achieves, Partially Achieves, etc.)

**2. Core Values Alignment (30%)**
How you embody ApexTech's five core values
- Peer feedback collected via 360° survey
- Examples and specific behaviors are documented
- Not just "what" you achieved, but "how" you did it

**3. Collaboration & Impact (20%)**
Contribution to team success and cross-functional projects
- Input from project collaborators
- Assessment of knowledge sharing and mentorship
- Impact on team morale and culture

**4. Innovation & Initiative (10%)**
Demonstrated creativity, problem-solving, and proactive improvement
- New ideas implemented
- Process improvements suggested
- Learning and skill development

### The Review Process

**4-6 Weeks Before Review:**
- Self-assessment questionnaire sent via Lattice
- Manager solicits peer feedback (3-5 colleagues)
- Employee reflects on goals and achievements

**2 Weeks Before Review:**
- Manager drafts review based on all inputs
- Calibration sessions with department leadership ensure fairness
- HR reviews for consistency and bias

**Review Meeting (60-90 minutes):**
1. Manager shares written feedback
2. Employee shares self-assessment
3. Discussion of achievements, growth areas, and career aspirations
4. Setting goals for next review period
5. Compensation discussion (annual review only)

**Post-Review:**
- Development plan created within 1 week
- Monthly check-ins to track progress
- Mid-cycle pulse check (3 months after review)

### Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

If performance falls below expectations, managers will work with HR to create a Performance Improvement Plan:

**Structure:**
- 60-90 day timeframe
- Clear, measurable objectives
- Weekly check-ins with manager
- HR oversight and support
- Coaching resources provided

**Outcomes:**
- Successful completion: Return to standard performance cycle
- Partial improvement: Extension with modified goals
- No improvement: May lead to separation

**Support Available:**
- Executive coaching
- Skills training
- Workload adjustment
- Temporary project reassignment

### Career Growth Opportunities

**Career Tracks:**
- **Individual Contributor (IC) Track**: Technical/specialist roles (IC1 through IC7)
- **Management Track**: People leadership roles (M1 through M5)
- **Lateral Moves**: Encouraged! Many employees explore different departments

**Promotion Criteria:**
- Consistently performs at next level for 2+ review cycles
- Demonstrates readiness through stretch projects
- Business need exists for the role
- Strong peer and manager advocacy

**Internal Mobility:**
- All positions posted internally first
- Employees can apply after 12 months in current role
- Encouraged to discuss career interests with managers quarterly

## Workplace Behavior

### Professional Conduct Standards

**Communication:**
- Respond to Slack messages within 4 business hours during work hours
- Set status when unavailable (meeting, lunch, focus time, OOO)
- Use threaded replies to keep channels organized
- Default to public channels over DMs for transparency

**Meeting Etiquette:**
- Arrive on time; notify if running late
- Video on for team meetings (cameras optional for large all-hands)
- Come prepared with pre-read materials reviewed
- Mute when not speaking on large calls
- Take notes and document action items
- End meetings 5 minutes early to allow breaks (25/50-minute defaults)

**Workspace Standards (In-Office):**
- Keep shared spaces clean (kitchen, conference rooms)
- Book conference rooms through Outlook; release if unused
- Respect "do not disturb" signals (headphones, desk flags)
- Visitor policy: All guests must sign in at reception

### Inclusive Workplace Practices

**Accessibility:**
- Request accommodations through hr@apextech.solutions
- Ergonomic equipment available for all employees
- Quiet rooms and wellness spaces on every floor
- Accessible parking and building entrances

**Diversity & Belonging:**
- Employee Resource Groups (ERGs): Women in Tech, LGBTQ+ Allies, BIPOC in Tech, Veterans, Parents & Caregivers
- Unconscious bias training required annually
- Inclusive language guide available in Notion
- Pronouns encouraged (but not required) in email signatures and Slack profiles

**Religious & Cultural Observance:**
- Flexible PTO supports all religious holidays
- Prayer/meditation rooms available
- Dietary accommodations for company meals
- Cultural celebration events throughout the year

### Dress Code

**Office Environment:**
- Business casual is standard
- Customer-facing roles: business professional on meeting days
- "Dress for your day": adjust based on your schedule
- Fridays are casual (jeans welcome)

**Remote Work:**
- Dress professionally for video calls
- Represent the company positively in virtual backgrounds

### Prohibited Items
- Weapons of any kind
- Illegal drugs or alcohol (except at approved company events)
- Anything creating hostile or unsafe environment

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I meet with my manager?**
A: Weekly or bi-weekly 1:1s are standard. Use this time for status updates, feedback, career discussions, and roadblock removal.

**Q: Can I switch departments?**
A: Yes! After 12 months in your current role, you can apply for internal openings. Talk to your manager about your interests early.

**Q: What if I disagree with my performance review?**
A: First, discuss concerns with your manager. If unresolved, you can request an HR Business Partner review. Appeals are taken seriously.

**Q: How are promotions decided?**
A: Based on sustained performance at the next level, business need, and manager advocacy. Promotion decisions happen during bi-annual review cycles.

**Q: What's the difference between PTO and sick leave?**
A: See [HR Policies](hr_policies.md) for detailed breakdown. Quick answer: 20 vacation days (planned) vs. 10 sick days (illness/appointments).

**Q: Can I work remotely full-time?**
A: Depends on your role. See [Remote Work Policy](remote_work_policy.md). Many roles support 3 days/week remote.
