advanced rag techniques

Advanced RAG techniques improve retrieval accuracy, handling complex queries, and generation quality through pre-retrieval optimization (query rewriting, expansion), advanced indexing (hierarchical indexing, knowledge graphs), hybrid search (dense and sparse), and post-retrieval techniques (reranking). These methods enable production-grade AI applications by solving issues like noise, irrelevant retrieval, and poor accuracy.
